# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in Riga (later the Baltics) who need a thing for a day or a week (a drill, a tent, a projector, a bike) and would rather borrow it from a neighbour than buy it; and owners of rarely used things who want them to earn money. Both sides use the site on a phone as often as on a laptop. Languages: English (default), Latvian, Russian.

## Product Purpose

Yoyo is a peer-to-peer rental marketplace. A renter finds a thing nearby, books days, signs a contract with the owner, picks the thing up, brings it back, and gets the deposit back. Success for the first three months: the first real deals (target 100 listings and 20 deals without a dispute).

## Positioning

The deposit always comes back. Yoyo holds it until the owner confirms the return, auto-releases it after 48 hours, and a person reviews every listing before it goes live. The name and the mechanism are the same promise: like a yo-yo, it comes back.

## Capabilities

Accounts (email, Google), listings with photos and moderation, catalog with map search, booking with a calendar, contract, simulated payments with a wallet and a held deposit, chat and reviews (planned), admin moderation. Service fee 0% for the first three months after launch, then 10%.

## Constraints

- Payments are simulated and labelled "test mode" until real money arrives.
- No real listing photos exist for marketing yet; marketing surfaces must work with authored, synthetic content.
- Brand name "Yoyo" stays; naming is English-only (possible worldwide launch).
- The intro page is the public introduction to the project and must explain what Yoyo is; strong SEO is required.

## Voice

Plain, warm, exact. No hype, no exclamation marks. Verbs on buttons.

## Assumptions (inferred from the brief, not confirmed)

- Owner's design taste (confirmed in session 2026-11-13): night + cream + dark chrome + one ice-blue light; no yellow; references (weblove, textura, Buttermax) supply concept and motion, never colour.
- Nav must offer Log in and Sign up, because booking and listing require an account.
