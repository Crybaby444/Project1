# Yoyo — full audit and plan (2026-11-16)

Owner's brief (2026-11-16): a full audit of design, logic, security and everything else; what is still to
do, with how others do it; every scenario walked through; a complete working project where payments and
signatures are finished and only switched to stubs for now; a step-by-step plan, then do it item by item.

Status marks: **DONE** (merged or in the merge queue), **NEXT** (being worked on), **TODO**, **OWNER**
(needs the owner: an account, money, a contract or a legal decision).

## 1. Where the product stands

- **Live:** yoyo.rent (Vercel, fra1) + API (Render Frankfurt, free plan) + Postgres (Neon eu-central-1).
- **Works today:** accounts (email, Google; Apple ready but off), 2-step sign-in, profiles, follows,
  shops, listings with a 6-step builder and drafts, moderation, catalog with search/filters/map,
  favourites, saved searches, chat (deal and direct), price offers, the whole deal flow (request →
  accept → contract → sign → pay → handover → return → check → deposit), reviews, reports, disputes,
  notifications (bell + email in 3 languages), identity check by a moderator, admin panel (overview,
  traffic, SEO, moderation, identity, deals, categories, reports, users, money, audit, settings, system,
  staff, security), GDPR export and deletion, legal pages, SEO (sitemaps, JSON-LD, hreflang), a11y tests.
- **Tests:** API 729, web 826 unit, ~30 Playwright specs (deal flow, builder, moderation, a11y, phones).
- **Stubs:** payments (a fake card provider), SMS (off), Apple sign-in (off until keys).

## 3. Money and the law (the most important finding)

| # | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | **The wallet.** People top up a Yoyo balance and withdraw it to a bank. In the EU, a platform that holds users' money and lets them withdraw it issues e-money: that needs an EMI/PI licence, or a licensed provider holding the money under *its* licence. Stripe Connect keeps sellers' funds in Stripe's safeguarding accounts (the platform never holds them); Mangopay gives each user a wallet under its own e-money licence. | **Decision:** go live on Stripe Connect (Express accounts for owners) and show "your earnings" as the Connect balance; or Mangopay if a visible top-up wallet is essential. Code: TODO (B1) |
| M2 | **How others take money.** Hygglo (which absorbed Fat Llama): payment at booking through Stripe; owners pick one of three cancellation policies; refunds through Stripe; payouts after the rental ends (Fat Llama: 1–3 days after completion). Fat Llama verified *transactions* (more checks for pricier rentals) and insured items instead of cash deposits. | Plan for Yoyo: pay at acceptance (PaymentIntent: rent + fee + deposit), payout 24 h after the return check, deposit refunded then (or kept for a proven claim). Cancellation policies: TODO (D3) |
| M3 | **Deposits longer than 7 days.** A card authorisation (hold) lasts ~7 days, so a long rental cannot just hold the deposit. Charge it with the rent and refund it after the check; Yoyo already models the deposit this way in its ledger. | Matches the current ledger; B1 keeps it |
| M4 | **Fee and invoices.** 0 % now, 10 % later (owner's decision). Receipts and the platform's invoice for its fee need the company's details. | **OWNER** (company) |
| M5 | **Signatures.** A deal is signed by typing your full name; Yoyo keeps who, when, IP, browser and the exact terms (hash). That is a simple electronic signature under eIDAS: valid as evidence, weaker than an advanced one. Smart-ID / eParaksts / Dokobit give advanced or qualified signatures but need a contract with a company. | Code: a `SignatureProvider` with today's method as the default and a ready adapter shape for Dokobit (TODO C1); turning it on: **OWNER** |
| M6 | Insurance: Hygglo includes all-risk cover (30 000 SEK cap). Yoyo has only the deposit. | **OWNER** (insurer partner later) |

## 7. The plan, in order

1. **H1** Demo listings for Latvia: 20–30 items, full descriptions in 3 languages, 3–4 branded images each, one demo owner, removable with one command.
2. **E1** Admin → Activity: the user-activity log with filters.
3. **D1** Home "For you": recommendations by interests / popularity.
4. **F1** Profile redesign.
5. **G1** One grid: three containers, one vertical rhythm, every page moved onto them.
6. **A3** Server errors in the admin (no third-party).
7. **B1** Payments: Stripe Connect provider complete behind `PAYMENTS=stripe` (PaymentIntents, Connect Express onboarding, transfers, refunds, webhooks), fake stays the default; the wallet becomes "earnings".
8. **C1** Signature provider interface + Dokobit adapter (off).
9. **D3** Cancellation policies (flexible / moderate / strict).
10. **E2–E4** Admin: edit listings, a person's full history, bulk actions, exports.
11. **A5** Nightly encrypted database dump.
12. **D4** Value-based checks for expensive rentals.

Owner items (no code can do them): S5 email plan, S6 Render Starter, M1 legal confirmation and
the Stripe/Mangopay account, M4 company details, M5 signing provider contract, M6 insurance.
