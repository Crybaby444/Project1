What is Yoyo

Yoyo is a rental marketplace between people. Take what you need for as long as you need it, share what you rarely use, and everything finds its way back.

01

Find it nearby

Search the catalog or the map. Every listing is checked by a person before it goes live.

02

Book the days

Pick the dates and send a request. Nothing is charged until the owner accepts and you both sign the contract.

03

Meet and check

Hand it over with photos in the deal, so both sides see the state it left in.

04

Bring it back

The owner checks it, and the deposit goes back to you. Like a yo-yo.

How Yoyo keeps it fair

01 / 05
  • 0% service fee for the first three months, then 10%, shown before you book.
  • 48 hours to check the return, then the deposit goes back by itself.
  • One contract for every deal, signed by both sides before any money moves.
  • 100% of listings checked by a person before they go live.
  • 3 languages: English, Latviešu and Русский.
